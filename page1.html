P2P VIEWING AND GAMING CENTER

My Business Plan

My business plan is very simple. I want to use two things that already have crowd - football and gaming - to make money.

First, business is Football Viewing Centre and Jersey Business. I live in Lagos, and I know how football moves people. For every big Liverpool vs Man City match, at least 100 people want to watch. If I charge 500 Naira per person, that is 50,000 Naira for one match. If I show 8 big matches in a month, that is 400,000 Naira. And on top of that, I will sell Liverpool jerseys, caps and scarves outside the centre. One original jersey is 15,000 Naira, I buy it for 9,000 Naira, I gain 6,000 Naira per jersey. If I sell 20 jerseys in a month, that is 120,000 Naira extra. So football alone can give me over 500,000 Naira monthly if I am consistent.

Second business is Free Fire Gaming Business. I am good at Free Fire, I have been playing for 3 years. Many people in Lagos want to learn, want to buy good accounts, want to join tournaments. My plan is to organize a weekly Free Fire tournament in my area. Entry fee 1,000 Naira, 100 players join, that is 100,000 Naira. I give winner 40,000, second 20,000, third 10,000, I keep 30,000 Naira profit every week. That is 120,000 Naira per month. I will also stream my games on TikTok and YouTube, and I can make money from gifts and ads. And I will sell high-level Free Fire accounts.

When I combine the two, football brings the older crowd and family men, Free Fire brings the youth. Both of them meet in my centre. People can come to watch match and also play game before match starts. That is how I will build a community, not just a business.
